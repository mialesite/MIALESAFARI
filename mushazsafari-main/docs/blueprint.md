# **App Name**: Alvena Elite

## Core Features:

- Cinematic Story-Led Storefront: High-fidelity video integration and interactive storytelling layers that showcase the Arusha landscape and honey production process.
- Bespoke Wellness Consultant: An AI-powered assistant that acts as a tool to recommend specific honey varieties and apiculture products based on user health goals and taste profiles.
- Premium Glassmorphic Product Catalog: An ultra-modern shopping interface featuring high-contrast photography, layered glass cards, and immersive product-level micro-interactions.
- Environmental Impact Tracker: Dynamic visualization of community metrics like hives distributed and farmers trained, powered by a secure cloud database.
- Integrated Artisan Marketplace: A full-featured checkout experience including product filtering, secure payment processing, and dynamic inventory management.
- Hive-to-Hand Transparency Portal: Interactive section documenting beekeeping locations and inspection standards with animated scroll-triggered content.
- Personalized Luxury Accounts: Member dashboard to track sustainable impact, re-order natural products, and access exclusive Tanzanian export content.

## Style Guidelines:

- The primary color is Honey Gold (#F4B400), chosen for its rich organic resonance with the product. This is paired with an ultra-dark background (#111111) to evoke a cinematic, premium feel.
- An analogous accent of Burnt Amber (#E57C00) is used to provide depth to gradients, while a Forest Green (#2F5D50) is used sparingly for environmental labels.
- A font pairing featuring 'Playfair' (serif) for cinematic, high-fashion headlines and 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for clean, objective, and readable editorial body text.
- A luxury editorial layout utilizing wide-screen cinematic proportions, generous whitespace, curved organic wave separators, and glassmorphic card containers.
- Ultra-thin, elegant line icons that emphasize luxury and precision, mirroring the minimalist aesthetic of modern technology brands.
- Immersive smooth scrolling powered by Lenis, complemented by GSAP scroll-triggered reveals, floating pollen particles, and honey-drip liquid motion.
